PLUVIA

One drop of rain, one long way home.

SteerDrag anywhere, tilt, or use the arrow keys. Heavy drops fall fast and turn slowly.
SplitTap (or Space) to shed a piece of yourself and burst toward the tap. It costs water. The piece stays behind: catch it again if you can.
Wet pathsGlass that was just crossed stays wet. Follow trails to slide faster and dry out slower.

Drops

IceStops evaporation for a while. Slippery and slow to steer.
SoapAlmost no friction. Very fast, very hard to hold.
StaticA magnet. Everything close is dragged into you, the grime too.
GrimeSteals water and makes you dry faster. Split to shed it.
PollenOne hides on every surface. Carry it to the soil and it blooms.
FrostOn the frozen roof, still water freezes. Keep moving, or rock left and right to crack free.
